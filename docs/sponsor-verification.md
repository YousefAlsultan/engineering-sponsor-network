# Sponsor verification

## Source-check symbol

A source-check icon means the platform confirmed the listed program from an official or credible public source.

It does **not** mean the company is a platform partner.

## Company-confirmed badge

A separate company-confirmed badge is for profiles claimed by an authorized company representative and approved by moderators.

## Partner section

Only show a company in the prioritized Partner Companies section when:

```text
Platform Relationship = Partner
```

Never infer partnership from public sponsorship evidence.

## Research pipeline

```text
Discover
-> Extract
-> Classify
-> Verify
-> Score
-> Moderate
-> Publish
-> Monitor
-> Refresh / Archive
```

## Expiration behavior

- before/through deadline: current
- 1–3 days after deadline: Recently Expired
- after the 3-day grace period: Expired Archive
- archive rather than delete historical programs

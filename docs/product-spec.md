# Product specification

## Product statement

A sponsorship discovery and application platform specifically for **university engineering teams**.

The platform helps engineering organizations find companies that may support their builds, understand what each sponsor provides, determine eligibility, and use the appropriate application/contact route.

## Current public UX

The directory must quickly answer:
- Who is this for?
- What can the team get?
- What happens when Apply is clicked?

## Trust model

There are separate concepts:
- **Source checked:** public evidence of the sponsorship/support program was confirmed.
- **Company confirmed:** an authorized representative claimed and passed moderation.
- **Partner:** a real platform relationship; only records explicitly marked Partner should appear in the prioritized partner section.
- **Sponsored placement:** future paid placement, always visibly labeled and not mixed into organic ranking deceptively.

## U.S. MVP

The initial public marketplace is U.S.-focused. Programs marked United States, North America, or Global may be relevant to the U.S. MVP.

## Application route rule

If a verified/approved sponsor contact route exists:
- show **Apply for Sponsorship**
- collect the structured request in-platform

If it does not:
- show **View Official Program**
- do not pretend the platform delivered an application
